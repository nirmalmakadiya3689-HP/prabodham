Privacy Policy

Prabodham Academy · last updated 15 November 2026

In short: your planner, notes, scores and settings live in your own Google Drive (a hidden folder only this app can open) and on your device. We run no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell data. Prabodham Academy is an independent study companion run by the app owner. It is not an official app of any religious organisation, Anirdesh, Google, Spotify or YouTube.

2. Permissions we ask for

  • Google Drive (app data folder only) to store your data.
  • Google Calendar events (optional) for reminders.
  • YouTube read-only (optional) for search.
  • Microphone and camera for calls and voice, notifications for reminders. Each can be refused or turned off in your device settings.

3. Services that receive limited data

Google (sign-in, Drive, Calendar, YouTube, Firebase, Fonts), Google Gemini or Anthropic (only with your key), Internet Archive and Openverse (your bhajan search words), Anirdesh.com, Spotify and YouTube embeds (they may set their own cookies when you play), Metered (call relay), jsDelivr/cdnjs (maths display library). Each has its own privacy policy.

4. Safety in chat

You can block people, report a person or group, leave groups and delete your chats at any time. Reports go to the contact address. We may remove accounts that harass others or share unlawful or hateful material.

5. Deleting your data

  • In the app: HQ → Safety & legal → Delete my account & data. This removes your Drive app-data folder contents, your chat directory entry, your group memberships, queued messages, calendar events the app created, and data on that device.
  • Without the app: email the contact address from your Google account address and ask for deletion. You can also remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all access.

5. Children

The app is meant for students aged 13 and over. Younger children should use it only with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Your data stays in your Google account, protected by Google’s security. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong Google password and 2-step verification.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the app shows the new version. Questions: the contact address.
